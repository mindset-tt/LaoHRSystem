# 13 — Reporting Center

## Existing
`/reports` page with NSSF report download (existing `ReportsController`).

## Phase 3C3 additions
Analytics dashboards are the primary reporting surface. The reporting center remains the home for regulatory/export reports (NSSF, PIT) — no new report designer.

## Standard reports
Curated operational reports only (no drag/drop report builder). Saved filters / column selection / scheduled reports deferred.

## Government reporting
NSSF/PIT reports are clearly labeled as internal summaries, NOT official filing formats (Phase 2C did not verify exact government formats).
