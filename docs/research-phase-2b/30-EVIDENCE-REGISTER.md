## 35 — Professional confirmation questions

### For a Lao labour lawyer

1. What is the exact statutory annual leave entitlement (days/year) under the current Labour Law (006/NOC as amended)? Which article?
2. What is the exact statutory maternity leave (days)? Is it 90 or 105? Who pays (employer, NSSF, or both)?
3. Is there statutory paternity leave? If so, how many days?
4. Is there statutory marriage leave? Bereavement leave? If so, how many days each?
5. What are the exact overtime multipliers for: (a) ordinary weekday, (b) night work, (c) rest day/weekend, (d) public holiday? Which article?
6. What is the monthly overtime cap (45h or 48h)? Which article?
7. What is the hourly rate calculation base for overtime (monthly salary ÷ what)?
8. What is the probation period (30 days? 60 days for technical roles)? Which article?
9. What are the statutory notice periods for termination (by service length)?
10. What is the severance calculation formula?
11. What is the foreign worker quota (percentage of workforce)?
12. What is the work permit process, duration, and renewal cycle?
13. Has the Labour Law been amended since 2013? If so, what changed?

### For a Lao tax adviser (ກົມສ່ວຍສາອາກອນ / MOF)

1. Confirm the current PIT brackets: are they 0/5/10/15/20/25% at 2.5M/5M/15M/25M/65M?
2. Is the LAK 2,500,000 tax-free threshold the only personal allowance, or is there a separate deduction?
3. Is there a dependant deduction? If so, how much per dependant?
4. Which allowances are non-taxable (transport, meal, housing, etc.)?
5. Is NSSF employee contribution (5.5%) deductible from taxable income?
6. How are bonuses taxed (same as salary, or separately)?
7. What is the tax treatment for foreign employees? Same brackets? Any surcharge?
8. What is the monthly PIT withholding deadline (day of month)?
9. What is the annual PIT reconciliation deadline?
10. What is the PIT filing format (paper, online, specific form)?

### For LSSO (ອົງການປະກັນສັງຄົມ)

1. Confirm: employee 5.5%, employer 6.0% — are these still current?
2. What is the contribution ceiling (maximum monthly wage subject to contribution)? Is it LAK 4,500,000?
3. What is the contribution base (base salary only, or includes OT/allowances/bonus)?
4. Is there a minimum contribution base?
5. Are foreign employees required to participate in NSSF? Any exemptions?
6. What is the monthly NSSF payment deadline?
7. What is the NSSF reporting format?
8. How does the 11.5% split across benefit categories (pension, health, injury, maternity, unemployment)?
9. What is the Social Security Law number and latest amendment date?

### For Lao banks (BCEL/JDB corporate banking)

1. Do you support salary batch/bulk transfer via corporate online banking?
2. What file format(s) are supported (CSV, Excel, TXT, proprietary)?
3. What are the required columns/fields?
4. What is the file encoding (UTF-8? Lao language support)?
5. What is the maximum batch size?
6. Do you offer an API for salary payments?
7. What are the fees for salary batch transfers?
8. What is the cut-off time for same-day processing?
9. Do you support inter-bank transfers in the salary batch?
10. What account number validation rules do you apply?

### For a Lao legal firm (data protection)

1. Does Laos have a dedicated personal data protection law? If so, what is the law number?
2. Does a cybersecurity law exist?
3. What are the data localization requirements for employee personal data?
4. Are there cross-border data transfer restrictions?
5. What are the obligations for processing biometric data (fingerprints)?
6. What are the employee data handling obligations under the Labour Law?
7. Has the 2025 Constitution introduced new privacy/data provisions?
