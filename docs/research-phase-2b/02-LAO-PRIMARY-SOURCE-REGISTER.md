## Key primary sources still needed

1. **Labour Law full text** (Law No. 006/NOC, 2006, as amended) — search Official Gazette for "ກົດໝາຍແຮງງານ"
2. **Income Tax Law No. 67/NA** (2019/2020) — search Official Gazette for "ກົດໝາຍພາສີລາຍໄດ້"
3. **Social Security Law** — search Official Gazette for "ກົດໝາຍປະກັນສັງຄົມ"
4. **Presidential decree 6 Aug 2025** (PIT threshold change) — search Official Gazette
5. **Minimum wage decree** (Oct 2024) — search Official Gazette or MOLSW
6. **Electronic Transactions Law** (2012) — search Official Gazette for "ກົດໝາຍທຸລະກຳເອເລັກໂຕຣນິກ"
7. **2025 Constitution** — BritCham Laos has a PDF (britchamlaos.org); article-by-article English text needed

## Recommended action

A **Lao-speaking researcher** should directly search the Lao Official Gazette (`laoofficialgazette.gov.la`) for the law titles above. The site is live and hosts PDFs of enacted legislation. This is the single most effective action to close the remaining evidence gaps.
