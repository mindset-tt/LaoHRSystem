# 17 — Candidate Privacy

## PII principle
Recruitment data (name, phone, email, address, employment history, education, CV, interview comments, salary expectations) is sensitive HR data. Access is intentionally scoped.

## Not collected
Religion, ethnicity, political affiliation, health condition, family status, biometric data, criminal history (unless legally/policy justified).

## Document access
Candidate CV is not globally accessible. Viewers: recruiter, hiring manager, interview panel (where permitted), HR/Admin. Project managers/ordinary employees must not see recruitment files.

## Retention
No invented statutory retention period. Configurable retention metadata/policy foundation documented as a professional/legal confirmation requirement.

## Right to delete/anonymize
Not auto-implemented. Architecture distinguishes business/audit records vs candidate PII vs documents (future privacy requirement).
