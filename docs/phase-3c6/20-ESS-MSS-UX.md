# 20 — ESS / MSS UX

## ESS (employee)
My Goals, My Reviews, My Feedback, My 1:1s, My Development, My Learning, My Career. No manager-private notes.

## MSS (manager)
Team Goals, Reviews Due, 1:1s, Competency Gaps, Development Plans, Training, Feedback. Scoped to direct reports.

## HR admin
Performance Cycles, Competency Library, Review Progress, Training Catalog, Talent Review.

## Navigation
`/performance` (grouped). No 15 top-level links.

## Empty states
No active cycle, no goals, no feedback, no development plan, no training — no fake runtime records.
